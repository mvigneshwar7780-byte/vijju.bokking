## Experience: IMAX with Laser and IMAX 3D
IMAX features custom dual 4K laser projection systems delivering high sharpness, high contrast, and vivid colors on floor-to-ceiling curved screens. It includes a 12-channel acoustic sound system and up to 26% expanded aspect ratio (1.90:1 and 1.43:1). Best suited for blockbusters and space action films.

## Experience: 4DX Motion and Environmental Effects
4DX synchronizes on-screen action with motion-activated seating (heave, pitch, roll) and environmental atmospheric effects including rain, wind, fog, lightning, bubbles, vibration, and scent. Not recommended for pregnant guests, patrons with heart/back conditions, motion sickness, or children under 4.

## Experience: Dolby Cinema and Dolby Atmos
Dolby Cinema combines Dolby Vision HDR dual 4K laser projection (1,000,000:1 contrast ratio) with Dolby Atmos 3D spatial surround sound. Auditoriums are equipped with premium motorized leather power-recliner seating.

## Experience: VIP Dine-In Lounge
The VIP Dine-In Lounge offers motorized luxury recliners, personal tables, warm blankets, and in-seat waiter call buttons. The menu includes artisanal pizzas, gourmet sliders, cocktails, and desserts delivered directly to the seat.

## Policy: Ticket Cancellation and Refund Schedule
- Tier 1 (Full Refund): Cancellations made 2 or more hours prior to showtime receive a 100% refund of base ticket price to the original payment method, or a 105% CineCredits wallet refund.
- Tier 2 (Partial Refund): Cancellations made between 30 and 119 minutes before showtime incur a 20% cancellation fee, with 80% refunded to CineCredits wallet.
- Tier 3 (Non-Refundable): Cancellations made under 30 minutes before showtime or after show start are strictly non-refundable.
- Non-Refundable Items: Online convenience fees, booking fees, and 3D glass charges are non-refundable.
- Concessions: Pre-ordered food items are 100% refundable if cancelled at least 60 minutes before showtime.

## Policy: Seat Exchange and Rescheduling
- Free Seat Swaps: Seat numbers can be modified within the same screen and showtime for free up to 45 minutes before showtime via the 'My Bookings' tab.
- Movie / Time Changes: Changing the movie title, date, theater, or showtime requires cancelling the current booking and reserving a new ticket.
- Ticket Transfer: Digital tickets can be shared with friends or family via the 'Share Ticket' button or Apple/Google Wallet without name changes.

## Policy: Digital M-Ticket and Entry Check-In
- Paperless Entry: Show the dynamic QR code on the mobile app, SMS link, or digital wallet pass at the entrance scanner. Physical prints are not required.
- Dynamic Security: QR codes refresh security tokens every 60 seconds; static screenshot captures are invalid at scanners.
- Paper Stubs: Physical souvenir stubs can be printed at self-service lobby kiosks by entering the 8-character booking reference code.

## Policy: Age Verification and Late Admission
- Lap Child Policy: Children aged 24 months and under enter free on a parent's lap for G and PG movies. Children aged 3 and older require a standard Child ticket.
- Rated R (18+) Admission: Valid government photo ID with date of birth is required. Guests under 17 must be accompanied by an adult guardian aged 21 or older.
- Late Entry Window: Entry is allowed up to 20 minutes after published showtime. Once the feature begins, late arrivals may be seated in rear rows until scene changes.

## Policy: Outside Food and Beverages
- General Rule: Outside food, takeout meals, restaurant containers, and outside drinks are prohibited inside the cinema building.
- Permitted Items: Baby food, infant milk formula, and medically required dietary items are permitted after brief staff inspection.
- Water: Sealed plastic water bottles are permitted, and free filtered water refill stations are located near all lobby restrooms.

## Policy: Auditorium Code of Conduct and Anti-Piracy
- Device Etiquette: Mobile phones and smart devices must remain silent with screens dimmed throughout the presentation.
- Anti-Piracy: Recording video or audio inside the auditorium is strictly illegal under copyright law. Auditoriums are monitored by infrared cameras, and violators will be ejected and reported to authorities.
- Disruptions: Harassment, persistent loud talking, vaping, or smoking inside theaters results in immediate security removal without refund.

## Policy: Bag Check and Security Screening
- Security Checks: Cinema staff reserves the right to inspect backpacks, large bags, and packages prior to entry.
- Bag Size Limit: Bags larger than 16 x 16 x 8 inches are not permitted inside auditoriums and must be stored in lobby lockers.
- Prohibited Items: Weapons, laser pointers, pepper sprays, fireworks, professional camera tripods, and illegal substances are barred.

## Policy: Accessibility and ADA Compliance
- Accessible Seating: Dedicated wheelchair spaces and companion seats are available across all auditoriums.
- Assistive Listening: Complimentary RF headsets are available at Guest Services for hearing-impaired visitors.
- Closed Captioning: Personal CaptiView display units and narrative audio description headsets are provided upon request.
- Service Animals: Certified ADA service animals are permitted; standard pets and emotional support animals are excluded.
